5. How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted using HTTPS/TLS 1.3
  • Encryption at rest: All database data encrypted by Supabase

Terms of Service

The rules that govern your use of GlamConnect — clear, fair, and specific.

1. What GlamConnect Is

GlamConnect is a technology platform that connects independent makeup artists ("Artists") with clients who need makeup and beauty services ("Clients"). GlamConnect is a marketplace — we facilitate the connection, booking, and payment between Artists and Clients.

⚠️ Important: Artists on GlamConnect are independent contractors, not employees of GlamConnect. GlamConnect does not control how Artists perform their services, what hours they work, or what equipment they use. GlamConnect earns a platform fee (commission) for facilitating the connection and booking.

By creating an account and using GlamConnect, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

2. Client Terms

2.1 Booking

  • You must be 18 or older to book services independently. Clients under 18 require a parent or guardian to make the booking.
  • Bookings are confirmed when payment is received and the Artist accepts.
  • You must provide accurate information about the service required, location, and any relevant health conditions (allergies, skin sensitivities).
  • You are responsible for ensuring someone over 18 is present during home service bookings.

2.2 Cancellation and Refunds

  • More than 24 hours before service: Full refund, no questions asked
  • Less than 24 hours before service: 20% cancellation fee retained
  • Artist no-show: Full refund + Rs.200 compensation automatically
  • Service quality dispute: Raise within 24 hours — GlamConnect mediates fairly

2.3 Client Conduct

  • Treat all Artists with respect. Harassment of any kind results in immediate account suspension.
  • Do not attempt to take bookings off-platform after initial contact through GlamConnect.
  • Provide accurate address and accessibility information for home service bookings.

3. Artist Terms — Independent Contractor

📋 Your Status: By joining GlamConnect as an Artist, you confirm that you are operating as an independent contractor. You set your own prices, choose which bookings to accept, control your own schedule, and may work for other platforms simultaneously. GlamConnect does not direct how you perform your services.

3.1 Artist Responsibilities

  • Maintain all verifications current: Aadhaar, police verification, hygiene certificate
  • Maintain a professional kit with unexpired, compliant products
  • Arrive on time for all confirmed bookings. Cancellations within 12 hours of service are penalised
  • Carry and use sanitised, hygienic equipment for every booking
  • Declare any products you cannot use due to stock or availability
  • Never take bookings from GlamConnect clients off-platform — non-solicitation clause applies for 6 months after platform exit

3.2 Artist Pricing

Artists set their own prices. GlamConnect provides suggested pricing ranges based on city and category as guidance only. GlamConnect never mandates pricing and never penalises artists for pricing above or below any range.

3.3 Commission and Payments

  • GlamConnect charges a 10% platform commission on all bookings — this will never exceed 10%
  • Payments released to artist within 3–5 business days of confirmed service completion
  • TDS deducted as required by Section 194C of the Income Tax Act when applicable

✅ Our 10% Commitment: GlamConnect commits publicly that our platform commission will never exceed 10% for standard bookings. This is not a promotional rate — it is our permanent policy.

4. Payments, Commission, and Refunds

4.1 Payment Processing

All payments are processed through Razorpay, a licensed payment gateway regulated by the Reserve Bank of India. GlamConnect never stores card details. UPI payments attract 0% MDR; card/netbanking payments attract 1.9% processing fee absorbed by the platform.

4.2 Surge Pricing Cap

⚠️ Surge Pricing Policy: During peak demand (festival days, major muhurath dates), Artists may charge a demand premium. This premium is capped at 30% above the Artist's standard rate. Any booking displaying surge pricing will clearly show "Peak Date Pricing" with the percentage premium applied.

4.3 Dispute Resolution

  • Payment disputes must be raised within 24 hours of service completion
  • GlamConnect mediates all disputes fairly — reviewing communication, photos, and ratings
  • Final resolution communicated within 72 hours of dispute filing

5. Safety and Prohibited Conduct

5.1 Zero Tolerance Policies — Immediate Permanent Ban

  • Any form of sexual harassment, physical assault, or threatening behaviour
  • Discrimination based on religion, caste, ethnicity, gender, or disability
  • Using fake identity or impersonating another person
  • Creating fake reviews or paying for fake reviews
  • Operating off-platform after initial GlamConnect contact
  • Using or recommending recalled or banned products

5.2 Anti-Discrimination Policy

🚫 GlamConnect has a strict anti-discrimination policy. No Artist may refuse service based on a client's religion, caste, ethnicity, skin tone, gender identity, disability, or any other protected characteristic. Confirmed discrimination results in permanent platform ban with no appeal.

5.3 Child Safety

Services for clients under 18 require a parent or guardian to be present during the booking and service. No images of minors may be published as portfolio photos without explicit written parental consent. GlamConnect is fully committed to the safety of all children on or interacting with the platform.

6. Content and Reviews

6.1 Portfolio Photos

Artists must have written consent (documented via the platform's consent system) from clients before publishing before/after photos. Photos of clients under 18 require additional parental consent.

6.2 Reviews

  • Reviews must be genuine and based on personal experience
  • GlamConnect actively monitors for and removes fake reviews
  • Confirmed fake review purchase by an Artist results in permanent ban
  • Clients may not post reviews for bookings that did not take place

6.3 Prohibited Content

Content that is defamatory, sexually explicit, religiously offensive, or contains personally identifiable information of a third party without consent is prohibited and will be removed immediately.

7. Liability and Disputes

GlamConnect is a technology platform facilitating connections between independent contractors and clients. While we verify Artist identity and hygiene certification, we do not guarantee the outcome of any beauty service.

7.1 GlamConnect's Liability

  • We are liable for: payment processing failures, data breaches caused by our systems, failure to process verified refunds
  • We are not liable for: quality of service performed by independent Artists, personal injury not caused by platform negligence, force majeure events

7.2 Governing Law

These Terms are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in [Delhi / Mumbai — to be specified at launch].

8. Account Termination

8.1 Termination by User

You may delete your account at any time through Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your personal data will be deleted within 72 hours (booking financial records retained for 7 years for legal compliance).

8.2 Termination by GlamConnect

GlamConnect may suspend or terminate accounts for violations of these Terms. Except for zero-tolerance violations, we will:

  • Provide written notice with specific reason for deactivation
  • Allow 7 days for the user to submit a written appeal
  • Review every appeal personally
  • Communicate the appeal outcome in writing with reasoning

✅ No Silent Bans: GlamConnect commits to never silently deactivating an account. Every deactivation will include a written reason. Every user (except zero-tolerance violations) has the right to appeal.

Child Safety Policy

GlamConnect is committed to the safety of every child who interacts with our platform.

✅ POCSO Act 2012 and IT Act Compliant — Lawyer Reviewed

🚨 Emergency: If you witness any child safety concern on GlamConnect, report immediately to our Grievance Officer at [email protected] or call the Child Helpline at 1098. We take all child safety reports with the highest priority and respond within 2 hours.

Our Commitments

  • Age requirement: Clients must be 18+ to book independently. Bookings for clients under 18 require a parent or guardian to make the booking and be present during the service.
  • No minor images without consent: No photographs or videos of anyone under 18 may be published on the platform without explicit written consent from a parent or guardian.
  • Portfolio review: GlamConnect reviews all portfolio photos before publishing to check for inadvertent inclusion of minors.
  • Service settings: Home service bookings for clients under 18 require a parent or guardian to be physically present throughout the service.
  • Artist training: All GlamConnect artists complete a child safeguarding module as part of their certification, covering appropriate conduct and how to recognise and report concerns.
  • No content targeting minors: GlamConnect does not market to or create content directed at children under 13.
