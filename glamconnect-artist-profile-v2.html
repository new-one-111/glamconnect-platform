About Priya

Priya Sharma is a senior bridal and specialist makeup artist based in South Delhi with over 8 years of professional experience. She trained at VLCC Institute and has completed over 380 bookings on GlamConnect, maintaining a 4.9-star rating across 142 verified client reviews.

प्रिया शर्मा साउथ दिल्ली में स्थित एक वरिष्ठ ब्राइडल और स्पेशलिस्ट मेकअप आर्टिस्ट हैं, जिनके पास 8 साल से अधिक का पेशेवर अनुभव है।

What makes Priya different

Priya holds a GlamConnect Vitiligo Coverage Specialist certification — one of very few artists in Delhi trained specifically in colour-matching and camouflage techniques for vitiligo skin. She also holds a North Indian Bridal Specialist certification, recognising her deep expertise in the specific makeup and hair requirements of Punjabi, Rajasthani, and UP-style bridal traditions.

Service Philosophy

Priya believes every client deserves to feel genuinely beautiful — not just made up. Her pre-bridal consultations are not a formality: she uses them to understand skin tone, undertone, outfit colours, jewellery, and the emotional story of the occasion before selecting a single product. Clients with skin conditions receive the same respect, care, and quality of service as any other client — without comment on their condition unless they choose to discuss it.
