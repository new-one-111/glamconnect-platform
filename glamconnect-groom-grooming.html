Questions
Frequently Asked
Will the makeup look natural? I do not want to look "made up".▼
Photography-ready makeup is specifically designed to be invisible in real life while looking perfect on camera. You will not look like you are wearing makeup — you will just look like the best version of yourself. Our specialists are trained in natural male grooming aesthetics, not theatrical stage makeup.
How early before the wedding should I book?▼
For the Complete Package, book at least 2 weeks in advance. For the Royal Wedding Programme (which starts 4 weeks before), book 6 weeks in advance. For muhurath dates (peak wedding days), book 4–6 weeks ahead — specialists are fully booked within days of major auspicious dates.
Are the specialists male?▼
GlamConnect's groom specialists include both male and female professionals trained in male grooming techniques. When booking, you can specify your preference. All specialists — regardless of gender — are trained specifically in male skin prep, beard styling, and photography makeup for men.
My family thinks grooming is not necessary for grooms. How do I convince them?▼
Show them your wedding photos. The groom who spent 2 hours on professional grooming looks significantly better in every photograph — and wedding photographs last forever. Professional grooming does not change who you are. It simply ensures the camera captures the best version of you on the most photographed day of your life.
What if the specialist does not arrive on my wedding morning?▼
GlamConnect's emergency replacement guarantee: if your booked specialist cannot attend, we find an equivalent verified replacement within 2 hours. If no replacement is available, you receive a full refund plus ₹200 compensation automatically. No bride — or groom — is left without professional support on their wedding day.
