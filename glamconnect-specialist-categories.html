🌿 About Vitiligo
Vitiligo is a skin condition caused by the loss of pigment-producing cells (melanocytes). It is not contagious, not caused by diet or lifestyle, and affects all skin tones. The condition is covered under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016 in India. GlamConnect artists are trained in cultural sensitivity and never make assumptions about causes or treatments.
